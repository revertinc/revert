---
title: "Workable ATS Integration"
---

Revert supports **Workable** (`spi/v3`) as a first-class ATS integration (`[REVER-51]`).

## Supported Unified Objects
- **Job** (`GET /spi/v3/jobs`, `GET /spi/v3/jobs/:shortcode`)
- **Candidate** (`GET /spi/v3/candidates`, `POST /spi/v3/jobs/:shortcode/candidates`)
- **Department** (`GET /spi/v3/departments`)
- **Offer** (`GET /spi/v3/candidates/:id/offer`)
- **Proxy** (`POST /v1/ats/proxy`)
