#### Obtaining Workable OAuth Credentials

-   Sign in to your [Workable Developer / Partner Portal](https://workable.readme.io/reference/using-oauth-2) and register an OAuth 2.0 application.
-   Set the redirect URI to `https://app.revert.dev/oauth-callback/workable` (or your self-hosted callback URL) and copy your `client_id` and `client_secret`.

#### Connect to Workable via Revert

-   Create an account on Revert if you don't already have one. (https://app.revert.dev/sign-up)
-   Login to your Revert dashboard (https://app.revert.dev/sign-in) and go to the Integrations section and click on `Create App` - `Workable`.
-   Enter your `client_id`, `client_secret`, and optional Workable subdomain/organisation URL into the App credentials and click `Submit`.
